\begin{frame}
	\frametitle{Methodology}
	\framesubtitle{Approach overview}

	\textbf{Goal:} meta-learned semantics that internalise properties inherent to turbulent flow phenomena.

	% Rationale (kept as notes):
	% - These properties appear in a variety of other chaotic dynamical systems, whose
	%   governing equations are numerically cheaper to solve than the full Navier--Stokes.
	% - DiscoRL benefits from diverse training environments, provided they are
	%   (in some intangible way) linked.

	\medskip
